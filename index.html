About Me

I write stories, poems, and novels, and right now I'm also working on my own manga. Some days I'm putting a whole world into a novel, and other days I'm squeezing a feeling into a few lines of a poem. My manga is a silent romance, a story where the strongest feelings are never said out loud. It's built around friendship, brotherhood, betrayal, and love, and how each one can change the others. Turning these characters into manga panels is the project I'm most excited about at the moment. Most of my ideas show up when the room is quiet, because I like the sound of silence. It gives my characters room to speak, and it lets me hear them clearly, which suits a story about love that never needs words. I'm also at home in the dark. My favorite color is white, but I love darkness, which is why a white spider on a black web feels like it was made for me. When I'm not writing, you can find me in a mall, eating buffalo chicken wings at a restaurant and planning what happens next in my stories.

To me, silence isn't emptiness. It's the space where my ideas come through. When nothing is competing for my attention, I can hear my characters and find the words for what they feel. Silence is also the heart of my manga, because the deepest feelings in it, like love, loyalty, and hurt, are the ones nobody says out loud. In the dark and the quiet, I feel most like myself.

My Favorites

Favorite color
White
White is the brightest thing in the dark. I love darkness, and white is what shines out of it.
Favorite food
Buffalo chicken wings
They're spicy, saucy, and always the best part of a trip to a restaurant. I plan my stories over a plate of them.
Favorite animal
Spiders
Spiders live in the dark and move without a sound, and they build something beautiful out of almost nothing.
Favorite sport
Basketball
It's fast and always changing, and one moment can turn a whole game around, like a plot twist in a story.
Favorite subject
English
English is where I get to read, write, and play with words, the same things I do in my stories, poems, and novels.
Favorite animes
Death Note and Attack on Titan
Death Note is a dark, high-stakes mind game between Light and L, and Attack on Titan has Eren, a morally complex hero with a fierce, unstoppable drive. Both build elaborate plots full of betrayals and twists, where every early clue pays off later.
Favorite art style
Digital watercolor painting
Soft washes of color blend and bleed into each other like a feeling that never needs words. Paired with deep shadow and a hush, it turns a quiet moment into something you can see, which is why it suits my stories so well.
Favorite person
My childhood best friend
She's the one I trusted the most, laughed with the most, and talked to the most. She's the reason my life got brighter. Even though I embrace darkness and silence, she slips in and fills my life with light and joy.
